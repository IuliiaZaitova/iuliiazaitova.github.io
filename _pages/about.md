I am Iuliia (you-lee-yah) Zaitova, an AI Research Engineer at [Diabolocom](https://www.diabolocom.com) and a PhD candidate in Computational Linguistics at [Saarland University](https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/department/lst.html) (thesis submitted, awaiting defense). I post-train and evaluate transformer-based speech and language models using SFT, RL, and DPO, and I design the evaluation methodology that checks whether those changes improve behavior, not just metrics.

## Current work

**AI Research Engineer, [Diabolocom](https://www.diabolocom.com)**, June 2026–present. I benchmark open multilingual TTS systems (Qwen3-TTS, MOSS-TTS, Higgs Audio) for call-center synthesis, focusing on dialogue-turn consistency, voice cloning, and inference-time pronunciation correction for named entities. I am extending language coverage with supervised fine-tuning, followed by RL-based post-training.

**Applied Scientist Intern, Amazon (Alexa TTS)**, July 2025–January 2026. I post-trained a production multilingual TTS model via SFT followed by RL, and designed the evaluation framework used to validate the resulting behavioral changes. I also built benchmarking datasets and automated diagnostics adopted by research and engineering teams for iterative model improvement.

## Research

PhD thesis: *Non-Compositionality in Cross-Linguistic Intercomprehension* (submitted, awaiting defense), [SFB 1102, project C4](https://sfb1102.uni-saarland.de/projects/mutual-intelligibility-and-surprisal-in-slavic-intercomprehension-incomslav/), 2022–late 2026. Advisors: Prof. Dr. Tania Avgustinova, Prof. Dr. Dietrich Klakow, and Prof. Dr. Bernd Möbius.

I work on post-training and evaluation of generative language and speech models (SFT, DPO, RL); multilingual representation learning and cross-lingual generalization; and evaluation methodology for generative systems (LLM-as-judge, pairwise preference, ablation-based diagnosis). The doctoral research focuses on non-compositional and figurative expressions, cross-lingual transfer, and speech–text intelligibility.
