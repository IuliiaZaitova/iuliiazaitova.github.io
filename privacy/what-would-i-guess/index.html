Privacy Policy – What Would I Guess?

Privacy Policy for What Would I Guess?

Effective date: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how What Would I Guess? (“the app”, “we”, “our”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you enter in the app

  • Player names
  • Game choices and answers
  • In-app preferences (for example, selected pack/theme and music preferences)

b) Account and authentication information (if sign-in is used)

If your deployment enables sign-in, we may process:

  • OAuth/account identifier (for example, openId)
  • Basic profile details provided by the authentication provider
  • Session tokens/cookies required to keep you signed in

c) Device and technical information

We may process basic technical data required for app operation, such as:

  • App version and platform type
  • Crash/error logs generated during normal operation

d) Permissions

The app may request notification permission on Android (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) if notifications are enabled in your deployment.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Run core gameplay features
  • Save session state and preferences
  • Authenticate users (if sign-in is enabled)
  • Improve app reliability and fix bugs
  • Share game results when you explicitly choose to share

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • Gameplay data is primarily used in-session and/or stored locally on your device.
  • If backend features are enabled by your deployment, limited account/session data may be stored on your backend infrastructure.
  • We retain data only as long as needed to provide app functionality, legal compliance, and security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We only share information in these situations:

  • With service providers required to run authentication or backend infrastructure
  • When you explicitly trigger sharing (for example, using the app’s share results feature)
  • If required by law or to protect rights and safety

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on your deployment, the app may use third-party services such as:

  • OAuth authentication providers
  • Cloud/backend hosting services
  • Platform tools required for app distribution and operation

Those services have their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

You can:

  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Revoke permissions (such as notifications) in device settings
  • Clear local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage

If sign-in/backend features are enabled and you want account data deleted, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International Transfers

If backend services are used, information may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above when changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact:

  • support@whatwouldiguess.app
